## Why the SLA Matters Strategically

CNLLC's biggest fear with a third-party vendor touching their customers isn't cost — it's **brand risk.** An AI agent calling leads under (or adjacent to) the Code Ninjas brand that says something wrong, gets aggressive, or breaks down = liability for CNLLC.

The SLA turns that fear into comfort: "Here are the measurable standards I commit to. If I miss them, here's what happens." It transforms you from "a franchisee with a side project" into "a professional vendor with contractual accountability."

**In the negotiation, the SLA is what you give. The IP acknowledgment is what you get.**

---

## The SLA — What It Should Cover

### 1. Uptime & Availability
| Metric | Commitment | Why CNLLC Cares |
|---|---|---|
| Service availability | 99.5% monthly uptime | Leads aren't missed due to system outages |
| Planned maintenance | 48hr advance notice, off-peak only | No surprise downtime during business hours |
| Incident response | Acknowledge within 2hrs, resolve within 24hrs | Problems get fixed fast |

*This costs you nothing — Retell and Twilio already run at 99.9%+. You're committing to less than your infra already delivers.*

### 2. Call Quality Standards
| Metric | Commitment | Why CNLLC Cares |
|---|---|---|
| Call completion rate | >90% of calls reach a meaningful interaction | Agent isn't hanging up or failing mid-call |
| Voicemail detection accuracy | >95% | Not leaving voicemails when a human answers, or vice versa |
| Escalation protocol | Transfer to live staff on explicit request | Humans can always reach a human |
| Prohibited language | No profanity, no competitor mentions, no pricing outside approved ranges | Brand protection |

### 3. Data & Privacy
| Metric | Commitment | Why CNLLC Cares |
|---|---|---|
| Call recording retention | 90 days, then auto-delete unless flagged | Compliance, storage liability |
| PII handling | No lead data shared with third parties | Privacy law compliance (PIPEDA, state laws) |
| Data ownership | Centre owns their lead data; TourForce processes, doesn't own | Clear data boundaries |
| Access controls | Each centre sees only their own data | No cross-contamination |

*This is critical for CNLLC. They need to know that customer data flowing through your system isn't a liability for them.*

### 4. Performance Reporting
| Metric | Commitment | Why CNLLC Cares |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly report to CNLLC | Calls made, tours booked, conversion rate, per location | Visibility without doing any work |
| Franchisee dashboard | Real-time access to their own metrics | Franchisee satisfaction |
| Quarterly review | Summary trends, improvements, roadmap | CNLLC feels in the loop |

*This is the "free consulting" that makes CNLLC love the arrangement. They get aggregated intelligence about franchisee lead performance that they've never had before.*

### 5. Brand & Compliance
| Metric | Commitment | Why CNLLC Cares |
|---|---|---|
| Script approval | CNLLC can review and approve agent scripts before deployment | They control what the AI says |
| Brand guidelines | Agent never claims to be "Code Ninjas" — positions as calling "on behalf of [Centre Name]" | No brand misrepresentation |
| Prompt changes | Material changes to agent behavior require 7-day notice to CNLLC | No surprises |
| Opt-out | Any lead can say "stop calling" and be permanently removed | Regulatory compliance |

*Script approval is the big one. It gives CNLLC veto power over what the AI says — which feels like control to them — but in practice, they'll approve your scripts because they work.*

### 6. Remedies (What Happens If You Miss)
| Situation | Remedy |
|---|---|
| Uptime below 99.5% in a month | Service credit to affected centres (e.g., 10% of monthly fee) |
| Material brand violation | Immediate suspension of affected agent, remediation within 24hrs |
| Repeated SLA misses (3+ months) | CNLLC can terminate approved vendor status with 60-day notice |

**The 60-day termination clause is the pressure release valve.** It gives CNLLC a way out if things go badly — which makes them more willing to sign in the first place. And 60 days gives you time to course-correct or transition.

---

## How to Present This to CNLLC

Don't send them a 10-page document. Give them a **one-page SLA summary** with:

- 6 categories (above)
- Key metrics in each
- Your commitment level
- Monthly reporting included

Position it as: *"I want to make sure this meets your standards. Here's what I'm committing to."*

The subtext: professional vendors have SLAs. Side projects don't. This signals you're serious.
