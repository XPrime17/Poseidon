# Agency Naming Brainstorm (2026-03-01)

## Status: SUPERSEDED — Name decided: TourForce (2026-03-02)
## See: `tourforce-branding.md` for the full branding bible (name, taglines, logos, colors, typography)

## The Vision
- AI transformation partner for subscription-based businesses (Code Ninjas, Kumon, Mathnasium, martial arts, etc.)
- Voice AI is the entry product; long-term = full platform (scheduling, retention, billing, marketing automation)
- Not just a tool vendor — a partner that embeds AI across the client's entire business

## Brand Identity (Uncovered During Session)
- **Value prop sentence:** "Like having an extra team member"
- **Core feeling:** Effortless (not powerful, not innovative — effortless)
- **Brand energy:** Warm, smooth, approachable — not industrial or aggressive
- **Key tension:** Market-specific names get meetings but feel small; broad names sound ambitious but don't signal "this is for me" to franchise owners

## Key Insights
1. Every "[GoodWord] + Force/Ops/OS/Craft" is taken — compound word formula in AI is saturated
2. The "narrowness" concern may be overweighted — strong brands outgrow literal names
3. TourForce remains cleanest available option found
4. "Effortless blend" direction (Meld, Alloy, Weave, Relay) is unexplored territory
5. An invented word (like Shopify, Twilio) might be the answer if every real word is taken
6. Previously registered as "Sub-Zero Automations" on Cekura (org ID: 3101) — will need to update if name changes

## Outcome (2026-03-02)
- 55+ names explored across 4 directions (wordplay, effortless blend, military/cool, invented)
- Every "cool authority" name (Prime, Axiom, Zero Drift, BlackBox) was taken by funded AI startups
- **TourForce won** — the only name that was available, distinctive, phonetically clear, had a double meaning, AND resonated emotionally
- Tagline: "Every call, a tour de force."
- Three logo concepts generated (Force Arrow, Shield, Wave)
- Full details in `tourforce-branding.md`
