## The Decision

**Name:** TourForce
**Domain:** tourforce.ai (AVAILABLE — not yet registered)
**Tagline (primary):** "Every call, a tour de force."
**Tagline (sales):** "Book more tours. Hire no one."
**Tagline (platform stage):** "Your operations, handled."

---

## The Vision

- AI transformation partner for subscription-based businesses (Code Ninjas, Kumon, Mathnasium, martial arts, etc.)
- Voice AI is the entry product; long-term = full platform (scheduling, retention, billing, marketing automation)
- Not just a tool vendor — a partner that embeds AI across the client's entire business
- **Value prop:** "Like having an extra team member"

---

## Brand Identity

### Core Archetype: Calm Authority
- Not warm/soft (not a spa) and not aggressive (not a defense contractor)
- The quiet operator who makes hard things look easy
- Like a chief of staff — runs everything, nobody notices until they're gone
- "We handle it" energy without shouting

### Scott's Taste Profile (derived from 55+ names evaluated)
| Dimension | Weight | Preference |
|-----------|--------|------------|
| Cool factor | HIGH | Proud to say the name |
| Authority | HIGH | Sounds competent, in control |
| Cleverness | MEDIUM | Double meanings appreciated (Sub-Zero, TourForce) |
| Warmth | MEDIUM | Calm > cozy. Not cold/clinical. |
| Scale ceiling | MEDIUM | Should hold a $50M company |
| Market signal | LOW-MEDIUM | Hints at AI/ops, not required |
| Effortless feeling | LOW-MEDIUM | Brand value, not naming requirement |

### Why TourForce Won
1. **Available:** tourforce.ai appears unregistered. Only conflict: tourforce.com (Australian tourism newsletter — zero market overlap)
2. **Double meaning:** "Tour de force" (excellence) + booking tours (literal product)
3. **Phonetic clarity:** Two syllables. Say it once, they remember it.
4. **Scale evidence:** Salesforce, Shopify, Mailchimp, Booking.com, Stripe, Slack, Amazon — all outgrew literal names. None renamed.
5. **Survived 55+ name gauntlet** where every "cool" name was taken by funded AI startups

### The Narrowness Concern — Resolved
- Scott's worry: "TourForce feels too narrow if I expand beyond tours"
- Resolution: The name doesn't define product scope — the brand does. Tagline evolves:
  - Stage 1: "Every call, a tour de force." (voice AI booking tours)
  - Stage 2: "From first call to lifelong member." (expanded ops)
  - Stage 3: "Your operations, handled." (full platform)
- Key insight: "Tour" has 4 semantic layers — guided visit, journey, tour de force, tour of duty

## Logo Concepts

Three directions generated. These are AI concept mockups — need professional vector refinement.

### Concept A: "The Force Arrow"
- **File:** `/root/tourforce-logo-concept-a.png`
- **Visual:** TF monogram — T's crossbar becomes forward-pointing chevron in electric blue
- **Vibe:** Modern SaaS (Stripe/Vercel). Forward momentum, precision.
- **Strengths:** Strongest standalone icon/favicon. Works at 32px.
- **Colors:** Deep navy (#1E3A5F) + electric blue (#3B82F6)

### Concept B: "The Shield"
- **File:** `/root/tourforce-logo-concept-b.png`
- **Visual:** Navy shield with gold compass rose inside
- **Vibe:** Premium, established, trustworthy. Military heritage.
- **Strengths:** Authority, premium feel. Compass ties to "tour" (navigation).
- **Colors:** Deep navy (#0F172A) + amber gold (#D97706)

### Concept C: "The Wave"
- **File:** `/root/tourforce-logo-concept-c.png`
- **Visual:** Bold wordmark with flowing indigo-to-teal waveform underneath
- **Vibe:** Modern, sophisticated (Linear/Notion aesthetic). Voice AI connection clear.
- **Strengths:** Most beautiful. Gradient adds warmth. Directly signals voice AI.
- **Colors:** Dark slate (#1E293B) + indigo-to-teal gradient (#6366F1 → #14B8A6)

### Poseidon's Assessment
- **Best icon/mark:** Concept A (Force Arrow) — works at all sizes
- **Most beautiful:** Concept C (Wave) — voice AI connection is clear
- **Most authoritative:** Concept B (Shield) — premium/established feel
- **Possible hybrid:** Force Arrow monogram as icon + Wave's typography and palette
