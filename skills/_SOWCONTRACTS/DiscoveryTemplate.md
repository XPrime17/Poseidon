# Discovery Questions Template

Framework for creating customized discovery questions for voice AI prospects.

---

## Document Header

```
Voice AI [Solution Type] Discovery Questions
Prepared for: [Client Company Name]
Prepared by: Sub-Zero Automations
Date: [Date]

The following questions will help us understand your business needs and design
the optimal Voice AI solution for [Client Company Name]. Please answer as
completely as possible — the more context we have, the better we can tailor
the solution to your specific workflows.
```

---

## Section I: Understanding Your Business & Current Operations

1. Can you walk us through what happens when a customer calls your business today?
2. How many inbound calls does your business receive on an average day?
3. What percentage of calls would you estimate go unanswered or to voicemail?
4. How many staff members are dedicated to answering phones? What's their hourly cost?
5. What are your current business hours? Do you receive calls outside those hours?
6. What's your average ticket/transaction value per customer?
7. How do you currently handle after-hours calls?

## Section II: Goals and Expectations

1. What would success look like for you in 6 months with a voice AI agent?
2. What's the #1 problem you want this solution to solve?
3. Are you looking to reduce staffing costs, capture more leads, or both?
4. What specific metrics would you want to track? (bookings, call volume, response time)
5. Is there a budget range you're working within?

## Section III: Tasks and Capabilities

Check all that apply — the AI agent should be able to:

- [ ] Answer inbound calls during business hours
- [ ] Handle after-hours calls
- [ ] Schedule appointments / book services
- [ ] Answer frequently asked questions (hours, pricing, services, location)
- [ ] Transfer calls to specific team members
- [ ] Take messages and send notifications
- [ ] Qualify leads before transferring
- [ ] Handle appointment confirmations / reminders (outbound)
- [ ] Process cancellations or reschedules
- [ ] Provide order/status updates
- [ ] Handle multiple languages
- [ ] Route calls based on department or urgency

## Section IV: Brand Voice and Customer Experience

1. How would you describe your brand's tone? (professional, friendly, casual, clinical)
2. Are there specific phrases or greetings you always use?
3. Are there things the AI should NEVER say? (pricing commitments, medical advice, etc.)
4. How should the AI introduce itself? (by name, as an assistant, as a receptionist?)
5. What should happen if a caller gets frustrated or demands a human?

## Section V: Technical and Operational Details

1. What CRM or booking system do you currently use? (GHL, Calendly, ServiceTitan, etc.)
2. Do you have a Google Business Profile or website with current business info?
3. What phone system do you use today? (landline, VoIP, cell, call service)
4. How many phone numbers need to be supported?
5. Are there any compliance requirements for your industry? (HIPAA, PCI-DSS, etc.)
6. Do you need call recordings and transcripts?

## Section VI: Reporting, Maintenance, and Escalation

1. How often would you want performance reports? (weekly, monthly)
2. Who on your team should receive call summaries and alerts?
3. What situations should ALWAYS be escalated to a human? (emergencies, complaints, etc.)
4. How quickly do you expect changes to the AI agent when you update hours, services, or pricing?

## Section VII: Success Criteria and Next Steps

1. What does a successful pilot look like for you? (specific numbers)
2. Who else needs to be involved in the decision to move forward?
3. What's your timeline for implementing a solution?
4. What concerns do you have about AI handling customer interactions?
5. Is there anything else we should know about your business or expectations?

## Niche-Specific Add-On Questions

### Dental / Medical
- Do you need HIPAA-compliant call handling?
- What appointment types should the AI be able to book? (cleaning, emergency, consultation)
- Should the AI collect insurance information?

### HVAC / Home Services
- Do you handle emergency/after-hours dispatch?
- Should the AI be able to provide service area information?
- What's your average response time expectation for urgent calls?

### Legal
- Do you need conflict-of-interest screening during intake?
- What practice areas should the AI route calls to?
- Are there privilege/confidentiality considerations for call recordings?

### Real Estate
- Should the AI qualify leads based on property criteria (budget, location, type)?
- Do you want the AI to provide property information from listings?
- How should the AI handle calls from existing clients vs. new leads?

### Fitness / Gym
- Should the AI handle membership inquiries and pricing?
- Can it schedule tours or trial classes?
- How should it handle cancellation requests?

## Strategic Probes (use during the call, not in written questions)

- "What's the cost of your current challenge?" — Quantify the pain
- "What would you do with the time your staff spends on the phone?" — Opportunity cost
- "What happens to your business when you miss a call on a Saturday?" — After-hours value
- "If we could show a 10:1 ROI in the first month, would that change your timeline?" — Urgency creation
