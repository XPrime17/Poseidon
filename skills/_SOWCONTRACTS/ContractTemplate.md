## 1. SERVICES

1.1 **Scope.** Provider shall deliver the Voice AI agent services described in the Statement of Work ("SOW") attached hereto as Exhibit A and incorporated by reference. Additional SOWs may be executed by the parties from time to time and shall be governed by this Agreement.

1.2 **Performance Standard.** Provider shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards for AI-powered telephony solutions.

1.3 **Service Availability.** Provider shall maintain a target uptime of 99.5% for the Voice AI agent platform, measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance windows communicated at least 48 hours in advance.

---

## 2. TERM AND RENEWAL

2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for an initial term of [12] months ("Initial Term").

2.2 **Renewal.** Following the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive [12]-month periods ("Renewal Terms") unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least [30] days prior to the end of the then-current term.

2.3 **Pilot Period.** The first [14] days following go-live shall constitute a Pilot Period. If the Voice AI agent fails to meet the performance criteria specified in the SOW during the Pilot Period, Client may terminate this Agreement and receive a full refund of the Setup Fee. Monthly retainer charges during the Pilot Period are non-refundable.

---

## 3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 **Fees.** Client shall pay Provider the fees set forth in the applicable SOW, including:
  - (a) Setup Fee (one-time, due upon execution of this Agreement)
  - (b) Monthly Retainer (due on the [1st] of each calendar month)
  - (c) Per-Minute Usage Fees, if applicable (billed monthly in arrears)

3.2 **Payment Terms.** All invoices are due within [15] days of receipt. Late payments shall accrue interest at the lesser of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law.

3.3 **Price Adjustments.** Provider may adjust Monthly Retainer fees upon [60] days' written notice, effective at the start of the next Renewal Term. No price increases shall apply during the Initial Term.

3.4 **Taxes.** Fees do not include applicable taxes. Client is responsible for all sales, use, and similar taxes arising from this Agreement, excluding taxes based on Provider's income.

---

## 4. CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

4.1 **Cooperation.** Client shall:
  - (a) Provide timely access to systems, accounts, and information necessary for service delivery
  - (b) Designate a primary point of contact with authority to make decisions
  - (c) Respond to Provider inquiries and review requests within [48] hours
  - (d) Provide accurate business information (hours, services, pricing, policies)
  - (e) Notify Provider within [24] hours of any changes to business information

4.2 **Delays.** Delays caused by Client's failure to fulfill responsibilities under this section shall not constitute a breach by Provider and may extend project timelines proportionally.

---

## 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 **Provider IP.** Provider retains all rights to its proprietary tools, platforms, frameworks, prompt engineering methodologies, and general knowledge developed independently or across multiple engagements. This includes but is not limited to conversation flow templates, testing frameworks, and integration patterns.

5.2 **Client IP.** Client retains all rights to its business data, brand assets, customer information, and proprietary business processes.

5.3 **Custom Work Product.** The custom Voice AI agent prompt, knowledge base content, and conversation flows created specifically for Client ("Custom Work Product") shall be owned by Client upon full payment of all fees. Provider retains a license to use general techniques and non-confidential learnings from the engagement.

5.4 **Platform License.** The underlying Voice AI platform, telephony infrastructure, and third-party services used to deliver the solution remain the property of their respective owners. Client's use of such platforms is subject to the applicable third-party terms of service.

---

## 6. DATA HANDLING AND PRIVACY

6.1 **Call Data.** All call recordings, transcripts, and customer interaction data generated through the Voice AI agent ("Call Data") are owned by Client.

6.2 **Data Processing.** Provider processes Call Data solely for the purpose of delivering, maintaining, and improving the Services. Provider shall not sell, rent, or share Call Data with third parties except as required to deliver the Services (e.g., AI platform providers, telephony carriers).

6.3 **Anonymized Analytics.** Provider retains the right to use anonymized and aggregated data derived from Call Data for service improvement, benchmarking, and internal analytics. Anonymized data shall not contain any information that could reasonably identify Client's customers or business.

6.4 **Data Security.** Provider shall implement and maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect Call Data, consistent with industry standards for AI telephony services.

6.5 **Data Retention.** Call recordings and transcripts shall be retained for [90] days unless Client requests a longer retention period. Client may request export or deletion of Call Data at any time during or after the term of this Agreement.

6.6 **Compliance.** Provider shall comply with applicable data protection laws. Where Client's industry requires specific compliance (e.g., HIPAA, PCI-DSS), Provider will implement additional safeguards as specified in the SOW and may charge additional fees for compliance-related requirements.

6.7 **AI Disclosure.** The Voice AI agent shall disclose its AI nature when directly asked by a caller, in compliance with applicable telecommunications and consumer protection regulations.

---

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 **Definition.** "Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by either party, including business plans, customer lists, pricing, technical data, and the terms of this Agreement.

7.2 **Obligations.** Each party shall: (a) protect the other's Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care; (b) use Confidential Information only as necessary to perform under this Agreement; and (c) not disclose Confidential Information to third parties without prior written consent, except to employees, contractors, and advisors with a need to know who are bound by comparable obligations.

7.3 **Exceptions.** Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving party; (b) was already known to the receiving party; (c) is independently developed without reference to the disclosing party's information; or (d) is required to be disclosed by law.

7.4 **Duration.** Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement for [2] years.

---

8.1 **Provider Warranties.** Provider represents and warrants that:
  - (a) It has the right and authority to enter into this Agreement and perform the Services
  - (b) The Services will be performed in a professional manner consistent with industry standards
  - (c) The Services will not infringe the intellectual property rights of any third party
  - (d) It will comply with applicable laws and regulations in performing the Services

8.2 **Client Warranties.** Client represents and warrants that:
  - (a) It has the right and authority to enter into this Agreement
  - (b) It will use the Services in compliance with applicable laws
  - (c) The business information provided to Provider is accurate and current

PROVIDER DOES NOT WARRANT THAT THE VOICE AI AGENT WILL BE ERROR-FREE OR THAT ALL CALLS WILL BE HANDLED WITHOUT ISSUE. ## 9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

9.1 **Cap.** PROVIDER'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID BY CLIENT TO PROVIDER DURING THE [12] MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

9.2 **Exclusion.** IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOST PROFITS, LOST REVENUE, OR LOSS OF BUSINESS, REGARDLESS OF WHETHER SUCH DAMAGES WERE FORESEEABLE.

9.3 **Exception.** The limitations in Sections 9.1 and 9.2 shall not apply to: (a) breaches of Section 7 (Confidentiality); (b) willful misconduct or gross negligence; or (c) Provider's indemnification obligations under Section 10.

---

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

10.1 **Provider Indemnification.** Provider shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Client from third-party claims arising from: (a) Provider's negligence or willful misconduct; or (b) infringement of third-party intellectual property rights by the Services.

10.2 **Client Indemnification.** Client shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Provider from third-party claims arising from: (a) Client's use of the Services in violation of applicable law; or (b) Client's breach of this Agreement.

---

## 11. TERMINATION

11.1 **Termination for Cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party materially breaches and fails to cure within [30] days of written notice.

11.2 **Termination for Convenience.** Either party may terminate this Agreement for any reason upon [60] days' written notice, effective at the end of the then-current billing period.

11.3 **Effect of Termination.** Upon termination:
  - (a) Client shall pay all fees accrued through the termination date
  - (b) Provider shall deliver all Custom Work Product to Client within [15] days
  - (c) Provider shall export and deliver all Call Data to Client within [30] days in a standard format
  - (d) Provider shall cease using Client's Confidential Information
  - (e) Setup fees are non-refundable except during the Pilot Period as described in Section 2.3

11.4 **Transition Assistance.** Upon request, Provider shall provide reasonable transition assistance for up to [30] days following termination at Provider's then-current hourly rate.

---

## 12. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

12.1 **Uptime.** Provider targets 99.5% monthly uptime for the Voice AI agent. Uptime is measured as: (total minutes in month - downtime minutes) / total minutes in month.

12.2 **Downtime Exclusions.** The following are excluded from downtime calculations: (a) scheduled maintenance; (b) force majeure events; (c) Client's systems or network failures; (d) third-party platform outages outside Provider's control.

12.3 **Service Credits.** If uptime falls below 99.5% in any calendar month, Client shall receive a service credit of 5% of the Monthly Retainer for each full 1% below the target, up to a maximum credit of 25% of the Monthly Retainer.

12.4 **Response Times.** Provider shall respond to support requests as follows:
  - Critical (agent completely down): [4] hours
  - High (significant functionality impaired): [8] hours
  - Normal (minor issues, change requests): [24] hours
  - Low (enhancements, questions): [48] hours

## 13. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

13.1 **Good Faith Negotiation.** The parties shall attempt to resolve any dispute through good faith negotiation for [30] days.

13.2 **Mediation.** If negotiation fails, the parties agree to submit the dispute to non-binding mediation before commencing litigation.

13.3 **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of [Ontario, Canada / or applicable US state], without regard to conflict of laws principles.

13.4 **Jurisdiction.** Any litigation shall be brought exclusively in the courts of [jurisdiction], and each party consents to the personal jurisdiction of such courts.

## 14. GENERAL PROVISIONS

14.1 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with all SOWs and Exhibits, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings.

14.2 **Amendments.** This Agreement may only be amended in writing signed by both parties.

14.3 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, except in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets.

14.4 **Force Majeure.** Neither party shall be liable for delays caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, pandemics, government actions, or third-party service outages.

14.5 **Notices.** All notices shall be in writing and sent to the addresses set forth in the signature block below, or as updated by written notice.

14.6 **Severability.** If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force.

14.7 **Independent Contractor.** Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, or agency relationship.

14.8 **Waiver.** Failure to enforce any provision shall not constitute a waiver of future enforcement.

## Jurisdiction-Specific Notes

### US Agreements
- Default: State of [Client's state] law
- Include TCPA compliance language if outbound calls are in scope
- Include TSR compliance if telemarketing is involved
- Add HIPAA Business Associate Agreement as Exhibit B if healthcare client
- Add PCI-DSS provisions if payment information is handled

### Canadian Agreements
- Default: Province of Ontario law
- Include PIPEDA compliance language
- Reference CASL for any electronic messaging
- Include French language provisions if Quebec-based client

### Other Jurisdictions
- Flag for local legal review before execution
- Research local data protection laws (GDPR for EU, etc.)
- Adjust currency and tax provisions
- Verify telecommunications regulations for AI voice agents
