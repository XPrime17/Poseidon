## Outreach Strategy

### Cold Outreach Framework (3-Touch Sequence)

**Touch 1: The Hook (Day 1)**
Subject: "[Business Name] — quick question about your phone"

```
Hey [Name],

I called [Business Name] yesterday and got sent to voicemail after 6 rings.

That's probably costing you 10-15 missed appointments a month.

We build AI phone agents that answer every call in under 1 second —
book appointments, answer FAQs, and never put anyone on hold.

Would a 60-second demo be worth your time?

— [Your Name]
```

**Touch 2: The Proof (Day 3)**
```
Quick follow-up — here's what happened when [Similar Business] switched:
• 43% more appointments booked
• 0 missed calls (was missing ~12/day)
• $4,200/month in recovered revenue

Happy to show you exactly how it works. 60 seconds.
```

**Touch 3: The Breakup (Day 7)**
```
Last note from me — I know you're busy running [Business Name].

If you ever want to stop losing calls to voicemail, the offer stands.

No pressure, no pitch deck, just a 60-second live demo.
```

### Warm Outreach (Referral / Inbound)

## Pricing Strategy

### Three-Tier Model

| Tier | Setup Fee | Monthly | What's Included |
|------|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| **Starter** | $500-1,000 | $297-497/mo | 1 agent, basic booking, FAQ, up to 200 calls/mo |
| **Professional** | $1,500-2,500 | $697-997/mo | 1-2 agents, CRM integration, transfer logic, up to 500 calls/mo |
| **Enterprise** | $3,000-5,000 | $1,497-1,997/mo | Multi-agent, multi-location, custom integrations, unlimited calls |

### How to Price by Niche

| Niche | Avg Ticket | Recommended Tier | Justification |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|---------------|
| Dental | $300-800/visit | Professional | High call volume, booking-critical |
| HVAC | $150-500/job | Professional | Emergency dispatch, seasonal spikes |
| Med Spa | $200-1,500/treatment | Professional-Enterprise | High ticket, multiple services |
| Gym | $30-100/mo | Starter | Lower ticket, simpler needs |
| Legal | $200-500/hr | Enterprise | Complex routing, intake forms |
| Real Estate | Variable | Professional | Lead qualification critical |

### The ROI Conversation

**Formula:**
```
Missed calls/month × conversion rate × avg ticket value = lost revenue

Example (Dental):
15 missed calls/day × 22 days × 40% booking rate × $350 avg = $46,200/month lost
Your agent costs $697/month → 66:1 ROI
```

**Key stat:** 62% of calls to small businesses go unanswered (Ruby Receptionist study). This is the single most powerful selling point.

## Demo Prep Guide

### The 60-Second Demo Script

**Setup (before the call):**
1. Build a live agent customized for THEIR business (use PromptBuilder)
2. Use THEIR business name, services, hours
3. Have it answer their actual phone number (or a demo line)

**The Demo:**
```
"I built something for [Business Name]. Let me show you.

[Dials the demo number on speakerphone]

Agent: 'Thanks for calling [Business Name]! This is [Agent Name],
how can I help you today?'

You: 'Hi, I'd like to schedule a cleaning for next Tuesday.'

Agent: 'I'd love to help with that! Can I get your name?'

[Let it run for 30-60 seconds]

That's what every caller would hear instead of voicemail.
It books directly into your calendar. Want me to turn it on?"
```

### Backup Demo Materials
- **Screen recording** of the agent dashboard (call logs, bookings)
- **Before/after metrics** from a similar business
- **Call recording** of the agent handling a real scenario
- **One-pager PDF** with pricing + ROI specific to their niche

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "It sounds robotic" | "Let me play you a call — [play recording]. That's ElevenLabs, the same voice tech used by [big brand]. It's indistinguishable from human." |
| "My patients/clients prefer humans" | "They do — and they also prefer someone answering vs. voicemail. Our agent handles the 80% of calls that are routine. Humans handle the 20% that need a personal touch." |
| "It's too expensive" | "You're missing ~15 calls/day. At a 40% booking rate and $[ticket], that's $[lost]/month. The agent pays for itself in the first week." |
| "I tried AI before and it was bad" | "Was that ChatGPT on a phone? We use specialized voice AI platforms — Vapi or Retell — purpose-built for phone conversations. Night and day difference." |
| "What if it makes a mistake?" | "It transfers to your team for anything outside its training. And we monitor every call for the first 30 days. You'll see transcripts of every conversation." |
| "I need to think about it" | "Totally fair. Can I send you a recording of your custom agent so you can share it with your team? No commitment — just listen and decide." |
| "Can you do a trial?" | "Yes — 14-day pilot. If the agent doesn't book at least [X] appointments, you pay nothing for the setup." |

## Qualifying Questions (Discovery Call)

1. How many calls does your business receive per day?
2. What happens when no one answers? (Voicemail? Missed entirely?)
3. What percentage of your revenue comes from phone bookings?
4. How many staff members answer phones? What's their hourly cost?
5. What's your average ticket/transaction value?
6. Have you tried any automation or answering services before?
7. What's your biggest frustration with your current phone setup?
8. If you could wave a magic wand, what would your phone system look like?
